# Authority Playbook

Earn direct editorial references through verifiable utility.

## Asset order

1. Reproducible flagship benchmark with corpus, method, raw results, limitations, corrections, and independent checks
2. Accurate reference chart or workflow guide that solves a recurring task
3. Formula and assumption sheet for a retained calculator
4. Public testing and correction methodology

Do not publish benchmark superiority claims before all review and reproduction gates pass.

## Prospecting

Build a small manually reviewed list of publishers whose readers have the exact problem the asset solves:

- web performance and CMS educators for image workflows
- privacy, legal operations, education, and nonprofit workflow publishers for local PDF tools
- financial-literacy educators for calculators only after source and assumption review
- libraries, universities, and public resource maintainers for durable reference material

## Outreach

- Disclose ownership.
- Lead with the useful evidence, correction, or workflow.
- Invite scrutiny and corrections rather than requesting a link.
- Personalize every message to an existing page or audience need.
- Stop a channel if replies show weak relevance.

Reject paid links, reciprocal ranking arrangements, generic directories, sponsored followed links, and mass templates.

## Gates

- Before outreach: reliable tool, approved claims, evidence asset, correction contact, baseline metrics
- After first 25-40 messages: require substantive replies or expert feedback before expanding
- At 90 days: retain only channels producing qualified referrals, editorial mentions, or relevant direct linking domains
