## Implemented intervention

- Added a visible, server-rendered overview below the calculator that states the annual conversion formula and that the fields should match the user's expected schedule. The calculator remains first so the supporting copy does not delay the main task.
- Added two concise schedule examples: `$25/hour` at 40 hours/week for 52 weeks equals `$52,000/year`; at 20 hours/week it equals `$26,000/year`.
- Added a bounded limitation: this tool converts gross pay only and does not estimate take-home pay, taxes, withholding, benefits, deductions, bonuses, overtime, paid leave, or local pay rules.
- Calculation logic, schema, tool registry, and metadata remain unchanged.

Proposed metadata wording for a later, separately measured registry change: `Convert hourly pay to annual salary, or annual salary to an hourly rate, using your hours per week and weeks per year. See gross daily, weekly, pay-period, monthly, and annual amounts.`
